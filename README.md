### Khanh Nguyen

Building **[dropto.run](https://dropto.run)**: from any AI agent to your client, and their comments back.

AI agents finish a page in ten minutes. Getting a client to sign off on it still takes a week of new
links, screenshots and feedback lost in chat. Drop2Run closes that loop. Your agent publishes the work,
your client comments right on the page, and the agent picks up the comments and fixes it.

**Solo-built, from idea to launch:**

- **Live in production.** Free to publish, with no account and no setup.
- **Works where people already are.** Publish from Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or any coding agent.
  Drop2Run is listed in [Claude's connector directory](https://claude.ai/directory/connectors/drop2run).
- **One place for the whole review.** Hosting, sharing, invite-only access and client comments all
  happen on the same link.
